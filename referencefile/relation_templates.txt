Relation templates (synthetic-friendly) for reversal / keying experiments

Format per relation:
  RELATION_ID
    Forward (fact) templates:    <deterministic statements using {S} and {O}>
    Reverse-question templates:  <questions that ask for {S} given {O}>

Notes:
- {S} = subject/answer entity; {O} = given/object entity (conditioning side).
- For toy experiments, prefer relations that are one-to-one or near one-to-one.
- Keep punctuation consistent if you plan to parse with regex.
