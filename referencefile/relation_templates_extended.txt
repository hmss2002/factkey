Relation templates (EXTENDED, synthetic-friendly) for reversal / keying experiments
Generated: 2025-12-22

Conventions
- {O} = the conditioned entity (known in the reverse question).
- {S} = the answer/value (to be predicted).
- Prefer deterministic / one-to-one facts in toy datasets. For one-to-many relations,
  either (a) pick a canonical single answer, or (b) change the evaluation accordingly.
- Keep punctuation fixed if you plan to parse with regex.

Template style
- Most relations are written as: "The <property> of {O} is {S}." and "What is the <property> of {O}?"
- Entity-to-entity relations are also represented in the same property-of style (e.g., CEO_of, capital_of).
